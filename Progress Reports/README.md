Progress Report – Week 2
To: ME4325 Lecturer
From: Austin Jinson
Subject: Co-op Placement Progress Report – Week 2
Project Overview
During my second week of placement, I continued gaining practical experience in the manufacturing environment. I was involved in investigating a fault with an automated conveyor system and its motor control.
Work Completed
•	Worked on troubleshooting a conveyor motor that was not starting from the PLC.
•	Checked the motor and its electrical connections.
•	Investigated the Siemens G120C inverter controlling the motor.
•	Checked whether the inverter was receiving the required Start and Forward signals.
•	Investigated the signals between the PLC and inverter.
•	Tested the motor and confirmed that it was capable of spinning.
•	Traced the system from the PLC through the inverter to the motor to identify the source of the problem.
•	Developed a better understanding of how PLCs, inverters and motors work together in an automated system.
Work in Progress
•	Continuing to investigate the control signals between the PLC and inverter.
•	Developing my understanding of the conveyor's control system.
•	Gaining further practical experience with electrical troubleshooting and industrial automation.
Work to Be Completed
•	Continue testing the PLC and inverter control signals.
•	Identify and resolve the remaining cause of the conveyor fault.
•	Continue developing my knowledge of industrial automation and motor control systems.
Complications
The main challenge was that the motor itself was capable of spinning, but it was not operating when the Start command was given from the PLC. This meant that the fault could have been within the control signals, PLC or inverter rather than the motor itself. I therefore had to test the different parts of the system to narrow down the cause.
